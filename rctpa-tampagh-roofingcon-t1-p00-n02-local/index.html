Understanding the Florida 25% Roof Replacement Mandate in Hillsborough County

Florida's building regulatory framework strictly governs roof repairs. Under Florida Building Code Existing Building provisions and recent statutory updates, when storm damage or deteriorated materials encompass more than 25% of a specific roof plane, partial patching is legally restricted. The entire roof assembly must be permitted and upgraded to modern wind-resistance standards. Frequently asked questions

Does my Florida homeowners insurance cover a full roof replacement after a hurricane?

Yes, if the roof suffered direct physical loss from a covered storm event (such as hurricane wind uplift, hail, or falling tree debris) and the damage exceeds your hurricane deductible, insurance carriers typically cover full replacement costs under replacement cost value (RCV) policies.

What documentation is needed to file a successful roof insurance claim in Tampa?

Essential documentation includes time-stamped pre- and post-storm photographs, aerial drone damage surveys, moisture mapping reports, emergency tarping invoices, and an itemized contractor scope of repair utilizing industry-standard Xactimate estimating.

Can I upgrade from asphalt shingles to standing seam metal or tile during a replacement?

Yes. Homeowners frequently pay the incremental material difference to upgrade from architectural shingles to long-lasting standing seam metal or concrete tile, significantly boosting property value, hurricane resilience, and insurance premium discounts.
