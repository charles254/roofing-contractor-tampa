Frequently asked questions

How long do architectural asphalt shingles last in Tampa's climate?

While manufacturers offer 30-to-50 year limited warranties, intense Florida ultraviolet radiation and subtropical storm cycles typically yield an effective operational lifespan of 18 to 22 years for premium architectural shingles in Tampa.

Can new shingles be installed over old shingles in Tampa?

No. The Florida Building Code prohibits "roof-overs" in wind-borne debris regions. All existing roofing layers must be torn off down to bare wood sheathing to inspect the deck and install code-mandated secondary water barriers.
