Tile Lift-and-Reset: Preserving Historic Tiles with New Underlayment

Polyfoam Adhesive Tile Attachment vs Mechanical Fasteners

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if my Tampa tile roof needs replacement or just new underlayment?

If you experience persistent leaks along valleys, eaves, or penetrations but your tiles are uncracked, your underlayment has likely reached the end of its lifespan. A comprehensive inspection will determine if a cost-effective lift-and-reset is viable.

Can I walk on my tile roof to clean debris or string holiday lights?

We strongly advise against walking on tile roofs. Concrete and clay tiles easily crack under concentrated foot pressure. If access is necessary, always step on the lower third of overlapping tiles using specialized foam walkway pads.
