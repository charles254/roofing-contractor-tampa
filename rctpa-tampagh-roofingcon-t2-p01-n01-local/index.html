Wind Uplift Mechanics & Shingle Sealant Strip Failure

During high-wind events, negative windward suction lifts shingle tabs and snaps the factory asphalt sealant line. Once broken, unsealed shingles flap and allow wind-driven rain to penetrate directly beneath underlayment layers, rotting plywood decking and saturating ceiling drywall.

Frequently asked questions

How long can an emergency roof tarp stay in place on a Tampa home?

A professionally installed 2x4 battened tarp is engineered to protect your structure for 90 days or longer, providing ample time to settle insurance claims, pull municipal building permits, and schedule full roof replacement.

Does insurance pay for emergency roof tarping in Florida?

Yes. Under standard property insurance policies, policyholders have a legal duty to mitigate further damages. Emergency tarping and water intrusion prevention expenses are covered under the policy's Reasonable Repairs provision.
