Tampa Roof Flashing Diagnostics: Step, Kickout, Valley Metal & Chimneys

Over 90% of chronic roof leaks in Tampa originate at flashing junctions rather than the field of the roof. Wherever a roof plane meets a vertical wall, chimney, dormer, or intersecting valley, custom-bent metal flashings must channel water away from the structure. We diagnose and replace failed flashings using heavy-gauge aluminum, lead, and soldered copper to ensure lifetime watertight integrity.

Kickout Flashing Installation: Protecting Exterior Wall Envelopes

Missing kickout flashings at roof-wall intersections allow water to run behind stucco, vinyl siding, and exterior insulation, rotting wall studs and breeding toxic black mold. We install custom-bent kickout diverters that direct concentrated roof runoff safely away from walls and into gutters.

Chimney Crickets, Valleys & Heavy-Duty Plumbing Boots

Standard plastic plumbing pipe boots degrade and crack after 5 to 7 years under Florida's scorching sun. We replace deteriorated flashings with seamless commercial-grade lead plumbing boots and install custom galvanized chimney crickets to divert water around wide obstructions.

  • Seamless lead vent boots that mold directly over plumbing pipes without rubber seals to rot
  • W-valley and closed-cut metal valley pans engineered for high-volume drainage
  • Custom fabricated sheet metal chimney crickets eliminating water pooling on high slopes
  • High-temp butyl sealing tape creating watertight mechanical seals beneath metal flanges
